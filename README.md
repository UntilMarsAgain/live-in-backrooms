## About Upstream Sources

### Selection of Levels, Items, and Entities

**The inclusion of any content must comply with this repository's
intellectual-property agreement.**

Levels, items, and entities from the original game are primarily sourced from
community articles on sites including but not limited to
[Wikidot](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/) and
[Fandom](http://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/), and may also be fusions or
original creations. Included content is not necessarily the latest version of
an article, not necessarily the version in its original language, and there is
no guarantee that the chosen version has not been deleted from the originating
site. The game may also adapt, modify, abridge, or fuse included content.

### Numbering of Levels, Items, and Entities

**Numbering should let players know the source of the corresponding content as
much as possible.**

Included content generally keeps the number assigned by its source. If two
distinct pieces of content share the same number and both are included, the
game may adjust their numbering, including but not limited to appending a 
suffix (e.g. `level-xxx-wikidot` and `level-xxx-fandom`).

For articles from language-specific sites, the number used by that language's
site is generally adopted — e.g. the
[Wikidot Chinese site](https://backrooms-wiki-cn.wikidot.com/) uses
`level-c-xxx` for its levels. Conflicts are handled by the rules in the
previous paragraph.

### Localization

Because translations and versions differ, localization follows the language of
the referenced original text, rather than treating the translation from a
corresponding language site as the sole reference.

For languages with multiple available translations, any version may generally
be chosen as long as it sounds better or fits the meaning.

If a language community has strong requirements about the correctness of its
community-established translated names, such matters are resolved by that
language's translators themselves; see the corresponding language's README.
The governing principle is that one thing in the game has exactly one name.
